OIRIA_OS goals
Updated 2026-11-11

DONE
----
- BMA400 accel driver: modular state machine, probe 0x14/0x15, CHIPID 0x90,
  ±1.8 g shake-to-wake on INT1, arm ESP32 light-sleep GPIO wake.
- Wake system: wake_reason_t enum (unknown, buttonpush, sensorping,
  genericInterrupt, power_restored, timer, alarm, periodic, ulp, force).
  Bitmask still carries multiples. Priority picker for primary.
- BMA force-wake tag: bma400_tag_shake_wake() → RTC force_tag
  (ULP_WAKE_SHAKE | ULP_WAKE_FORCE). ULP consumes it into wake_reason.
- Early boot: master-first (SOLO until after rescue window), then demote.
  Menu-held LCD+encoder mode select. Serial /bootseq rescue on UART0.
  Short listen window even if button not held.
- SSD1351 SPI driver (double FB, dirty rows, chunked push).
- Window highlight border gated on window_highlighted; focus raises layer.
- Vulcan IDE / VM port work already on main (separate from this list).
